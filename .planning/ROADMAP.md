# Doctor Genie — Roadmap

**Goal:** Launch a professional landing page for Doctor Genie that communicates the ecosystem vision, showcases AI capabilities through embedded demos, and drives CTA conversions (co-founders, partners, investors).

**Canonical refs:** REQUIREMENTS.md (full section list, visual identity, CTAs, video embeds, contact)

- Responsive landing page with 10 sections
- YouTube video embeds (4 videos)
- Hero with video background

- Mobile-responsive design
- Medical AI color scheme
- Modern minimal aesthetic
